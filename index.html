Official Flamecraft Rules & Artisan Abilities

1. Visiting a Shop

Move your Dragon token to any Shop in Town different from the one you started your turn on. If other players are present at that Shop, you must give each of them 1 Good or 1 Coin from your supply (unless visiting Flagons & Dragons).

2. Choose GATHER or ENCHANT

  • GATHER: (1) Gain 1 item from the Shop, each Enchantment, and each Artisan Dragon there. (2) Optional: Place 1 Artisan Dragon from your hand into a matching empty slot and gain the slot's printed reward (filling the 3rd slot draws a new Shop facedown). (3) Optional: Fire Up 1 Artisan Dragon at the Shop. (4) Optional: Use the Shop's special ability.
  • ENCHANT: (1) Select an Enchantment from the row (or your Reserve) matching the Shop's icon (or any icon at a Wild Shop; max 3 Enchantments per Shop). Pay the required Goods (Coins act as wild Goods unless marked with the No-Coin symbol). (2) Gain the Enchantment's rewards and tuck it under the Shop. (3) Optional: Fire Up ALL Artisan Dragons at the Shop in any order.

3. The 6 Artisan Dragon Specialties

  • Bread (Gold): Draw 1 Artisan Dragon from the Park or top of the deck.
  • Meat (Red): Place 1 Artisan Dragon from your hand into any matching empty slot in Town and gain its slot reward.
  • Iron (Dark Blue): Gain 2 of the same Good produced by the Shop or an Artisan Dragon there.
  • Crystal (Light Blue): Gain 3 different Goods of your choice.
  • Plant (Green): Gift 1 Good or Coin to another player to gain 2 Reputation.
  • Potion (Purple): Swap that Dragon with any other Artisan Dragon in Town (at a different Shop) or the Park (no slot reward).

4. End of Turn & Game End

Reveal any facedown new Shops, discard down to at most 6 Artisan Dragons and 7 of each Good (Coins are unlimited), and refill the Park and Enchantment row to 5 cards. When the last card of the Artisan Deck or Enchantment Deck is drawn, finish the current round so all players have equal turns. Leftover Coins score 1 Reputation each before scoring Moon Fancy Dragons.
